What we catch

The listing says “cash-flowing.” The details say otherwise.

Real examples from a single sweep of Florida listings on Nov 1, 2026.

Permits

New Smyrna Beach house + studio, $276K

Priced like a two-unit income property. But the studio may not be a permitted second unit. If it isn't legal, part of the rent, and part of the value, may not exist.

We check: zoning, permit history, and whether the second unit can be rented legally.

Unit count

Lake Worth Beach duplex: 9 bedrooms in 1,923 sq ft

That is a lot of bedrooms for the space. Extra rooms are often carved out without permits, and the legal unit and bedroom count drives rent, insurance and resale.

We check: legal unit count against permits and county records.

Cap-rate math

Hollywood duplex: “7.7% cap” is closer to 3.6%

The landlord pays water, one electric meter and gas. Once utilities and realistic expenses are counted, the return is less than half of what was advertised.

We check: who pays what, then redo the cap rate with honest numbers.

FAQ

Questions investors ask

Is the engineering screen a home inspection?

No. It is a desk review by a licensed professional engineer, done before you offer. It looks at records and listing data, such as permits, flood zone, roof age, sewer or septic, and condo milestone status, to flag likely hidden costs. It does not replace a home inspection, wind-mitigation report or other inspections you may need under contract.

How do you estimate cap rates?

We start with listed or estimated market rents, then subtract realistic expenses: taxes, insurance, maintenance, vacancy, management, and any utilities the landlord pays. Every deal card shows its basis so you can check the math. They are estimates, not investment advice.

Which areas do you cover?

Florida 2–4 unit income properties, with a focus on South Florida and the east coast. The feed may include other Florida markets when the numbers stand out.

Do I have to use you as my agent?

No. Deal Alerts, the Pre-Offer Engineering Screen and the Value-Add Finder are available on their own. Engineering fees are billed separately and do not depend on whether you close.

What if you represent me and also review the property?

Any dual role is disclosed to you in writing up front. Real estate services are provided through Hardeep's employing brokerage, and engineering work is billed separately.

Is this investment advice?

No. Deal figures are estimates to help you decide what to look at closely. Do your own due diligence and talk to your lender, insurer, attorney and tax advisor.
